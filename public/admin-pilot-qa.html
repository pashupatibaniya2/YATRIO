What this page does not assume

  • A booking is not treated as a successful payment.
  • Demo records and placeholder imagery are not treated as verified operator evidence.
  • Payment credentials, WhatsApp delivery and real wallet refunds require provider-side configuration and confirmation.
  • A successful API health check is not proof of a complete end-to-end booking.
