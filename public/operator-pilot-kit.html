Nepal · First operator pilot

Prepare once. Launch with confidence.

A practical preparation sheet for a bus company interested in joining YATRIO. Use it to organise your verification documents, real fleet evidence, driver details and first passenger-ready departure.

Start application →
Important — this is a preparation checklist, not an application or approval. Completing this sheet does not submit information to YATRIO, verify a business, activate an operator, publish a bus or guarantee bookings. Tick boxes and typed details stay in this browser page only and are not sent to YATRIO.

01 · Business verification pack

Prepare clear, current copies. The operator portal accepts PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 5 MB per file.

02 · Company contact details

Use the details that match your official business records.

03 · Real fleet evidence

Prepare details for each actual bus you intend to operate through YATRIO.

04 · Driver and operations

Only add drivers who have agreed to be assigned and whose credentials are valid.

05 · First route and departure worksheet

Plan one genuine service that your company is authorised and ready to operate. Do not advertise an unconfirmed schedule.
