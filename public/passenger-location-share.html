Share your pickup location

Let the operator handling your bus booking see your current pickup position so they can coordinate your boarding.

Your privacy matters. Sharing is optional and off by default. Your location is shared only with the operator for the selected booking, never on public trip links. It automatically expires 30 minutes after scheduled departure. You can stop sharing at any time.

Sign in to manage pickup location sharing.
How it works
  1. Choose an eligible upcoming bus booking.
  2. Tap “I agree” and allow GPS access in your browser.
  3. Keep this page open while coordinating your pickup.
  4. Tap “Stop sharing” whenever you no longer want to share.

YATRIO sends periodic location updates while this page is active. Closing the browser may pause updates; it does not revoke an existing share, so use Stop sharing before leaving this page.
