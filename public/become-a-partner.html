Help travellers choose confidently

Upload photos of the buses they will actually board.

After approval, add genuine photos for each bus: a clear exterior view, the passenger cabin, the actual seat layout and any amenities you list. Use unedited, current photos of your own vehicles.

Each bus can have up to 12 JPEG, PNG or WebP photos, up to 5 MB each. Uploads remain private until YATRIO reviews and approves them.

Frequently asked questions

Before you join

Is applying free?

The current YATRIO operator application page does not list an application fee. Confirm any commercial terms directly with YATRIO before relying on them.

How long does verification take?

Verification timing is not currently guaranteed. Your application remains pending until the required documents have been reviewed.

Can I publish schedules immediately?

No. The operator account must first be approved. All four required documents must be approved before the operator workspace is activated.

Are the photos on this page real partner buses?

No. The bus images on this page are illustrative examples only. Upload your own genuine fleet photographs after your account is approved.

Does approval mean payments and bookings are fully live?

Approval enables the operator workspace and schedule management subject to current platform functionality. Payment collection and settlement availability are separate and are not represented as live here.
