Bus cancellation & refund policy

The cancellation fee is based on the time remaining until the bus's scheduled departure. The estimate uses the verified amount paid for the bus booking.

Time before departureCancellation feeEstimated refund
48 hours or more10%90%
24 to under 48 hours20%80%
12 to under 24 hours30%70%
6 to under 12 hours50%50%
2 to under 6 hours75%25%
Under 2 hours100%0%

Important: Submitting a cancellation request does not cancel the confirmed ticket or transfer money. YATRIO reviews the request; the final refund depends on payment verification and provider processing. Wallet refunds are not automatically initiated by this policy. If departure has started or passed, contact support.

Marketplace stays, rides and experiences may have separate provider-specific cancellation terms.
